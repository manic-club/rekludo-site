How it plays

Three steps. No host. No teams.

  1. 1

    Everyone guesses

    Pass the phone round the table. Each player locks in a number in secret.

  2. 2

    The crowd appears

    Put the phone in the middle. The crowd's average drops in first, then the true answer.

  3. 3

    Beat the crowd

    Players land one by one, farthest first. Closer to the truth than the crowd? You score. Closest of all? Bonus point. And if nobody beats the crowd, the crowd takes it.

The lingo

You'll be saying these at the table

Bang on
Exactly right, or so close it makes no difference.
Lowball
Ten times too low, or worse.
Moonshot
Ten times too high, or worse.
Crowd pleaser
Whoever landed closest to the crowd's own average.
Crowd point
Nobody beat the crowd? Then the crowd scores.
