How it plays

Three steps. No host. No teams.

  1. 1

    Guess a number

    A question pops up. The answer is always a number. Everyone types a guess, one at a time. Pass the phone, keep it secret.

    For example: In what year was the Bitcoin white paper published?

  2. 2

    See the crowd

    Put the phone down. The app adds up all the guesses and shows the average. That's "the crowd". Then it shows the real answer.

  3. 3

    Get closer

    Closer to the answer than the crowd? You score. Closest of everyone? Bonus point. Nobody closer than the crowd? The crowd scores.

The secret ingredient

The wisdom of the crowd

Many guesses→One average→Near the truth

Some guesses are too low. Some are too high. Mix them all together and the mistakes cancel out.

That average is the crowd. And the crowd is hard to beat. Can you?
